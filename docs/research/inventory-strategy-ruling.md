# Ruling on the additional inventory strategies

**Historical implementation scope:** the later user-authorized
[six-strategy extension](../six-strategies.md) implements the four experiments deferred below.
The scientific cautions and limits on attribution still apply.

2026-09-06. The user supplied Daniro's full August 19 article and nine relevant screenshots,
and delegated the decision about adding strategies. This ruling supplements the original repair.

## Adopt the mechanisms that already have a clear purpose

Keep independent contract valuation, full-depth execution cost, fees, small fixed spending,
partial-fill accounting, persistent exits and global exposure limits. These are implemented in
the repaired bot. The article's examples do not specify fitted probability weights or establish
that its equations are the private algorithms of the displayed wallets.

The full research report verifies named public profiles and venue-reported profits, examines
primary research and positive/negative operator reports, and separates the AdanaKebab screenshots
from the August 19 article. No public dataset or reproducible ten-million-execution analysis was
found. That is a bounded search result, not proof that the claimed analysis never happened.

## Defer additional funded policies

Retain two recorded candidate modes and one selected executable mode. Do not add six funded
portfolios, a passive-order engine, automatic complete-set merging or an LLM trading loop in this
repair. The user authorized this choice. This is an engineering prioritization, not a finding
that these other strategies cannot work.

A same-time public quote comparison found 642 accepted directional comparisons from 332 matched
book pairs in two conditions (310 pairs supplied both directions, 22 one). Every comparison gave
identical calculated proceeds from direct SELL and buying the complementary token at five-share
depth, under current V2 cash-additive crypto fees and before merge/funding costs. The sample covers
2026-09-06 19:22:04–19:28:38 UTC; it contains correlated quotes, no fills and no profit observations.
Matching source timestamps does not guarantee simultaneous execution; maximum receipt separation
was 91 ms. The result supports deferring additional closing machinery, not rejecting maker or

An owned Up contract can be sold at s, or combined with a newly bought Down at d for terminal
value 1. Before other costs, compare s minus sell fee with 1 minus d minus buy fee. Historical
entry cost cancels in this route choice. Buying Down reduces exposure; it needs extra cash until
cash is released and does not create an extra forecasting edge merely by changing the label.

## A staged six-policy research menu

| Candidate | Distinct hypothesis | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| Modified momentum | A sufficiently large late BTC lead is useful after costs | Implemented comparator |
| Volatility/time value | Conditional contract valuation improves price selection | Implemented default; uncalibrated |
| Faster-information value | Aligned exchange information improves on delayed Chainlink inputs | Next research priority; no new feed added |
| Model-based exit | Updated holding value improves on fixed stop/target | Deferred; needs final averaging-minute model |
| Fixed-size passive sequential pairs | Earn spread/price reversal while explicitly pricing unmatched losses | Deferred; needs resting-order, cancel and merge lifecycles |
| Inventory-sensitive passive pairs | Adjusting quotes to existing imbalance improves the fixed-size policy | Deferred; compare against the preceding policy, not a different market/sample |

This menu is a plan, not six implemented portfolios or six independent sources of return. A USD100
allocation split six ways leaves about USD16.67 each and quickly runs into minimum sizes and hedge
funding. Start with one explicit small live configuration once account readiness is checked, using
actual fills and the existing journal. No mandatory paper simulator. Add competitors in stages;
record every round, reject, partial fill, residual holding and cash cost. Freeze rules before later
comparison, keep common capital/loss limits, and count unique market rounds rather than executions.

The cost of deferral is missing opportunities from passive orders or faster feeds. The benefit is
avoiding unverified order/cash lifecycles and a misleading six-way profit contest. Promote a new
policy when it has a distinct falsifiable hypothesis, usable inputs and measurable net execution
advantage or improved later outcomes. No promised ROI or automatic scale-up threshold is implied.
